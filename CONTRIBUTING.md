# Contributing

Thanks for helping. Bug reports and pull requests are welcome on GitHub.

## Licensing of contributions

Twofold is licensed under the GNU GPL v3.0, and the maintainer may one day also publish it on the Mac App Store, whose terms are not compatible with the GPL. Nothing is decided, but to keep that possible, contributions need one extra permission.

By submitting a pull request, you agree that:

1. your contribution is your own work, or you have the right to submit it;
2. it is licensed to everyone under the GPL v3.0, like the rest of the project; and
3. you also grant the project maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use, modify and distribute your contribution under other terms, including in a Mac App Store version of Twofold.

You keep the copyright in your work. This does not let anyone else sell your code outside the GPL; it only lets the official app be published on the App Store, should that happen.
